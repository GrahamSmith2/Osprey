## Why these lines

**Energy storage is a testing line, not a power line.** The pack set is
adequate for a race. It is not adequate for a season of testing: the previous
team skipped range testing entirely rather than risk draining packs to damage.
Doubling the pack count turns a single run per afternoon into a full test day,
which is the direct route to the in-water testing the Risk rubric now rewards.

**Propellers are the cheapest speed on the boat.** Osprey drew 45 A at 30 mph
where the powertrain was designed for roughly 140 A. The motors and controllers
are not the limit — the propeller is not loading them. A $200 set of pitches and
diameters is the highest-return line on this form.

**The driveline spares are against a known failure.** The flex shaft is retained
in its collet by clamping force alone, and at PEP26 it worked loose mid-race.
We are redesigning the retention and will need parts to iterate.

**Autonomy is new spend.** Osprey has never driven itself. The autopilot, GPS
and telemetry are the division requirement, including the kill-on-navigation-loss
the rules require us to demonstrate.

## Castle Creations sponsorship

We are approaching Castle Creations for two 2535 680Kv motors and two Hydra
Cobra 5 HV marine ESCs. Osprey runs their powertrain already and their Castle
Link logging is what gave us the data we are working from.

**Status: requested, not committed.** If it does not come through we absorb the
$1,480 as follows: reduce the pack purchase from eight to four (−$560) and
release the contingency (−$630), covering $1,190. The remaining **$290** comes
from club funds, which we hold independently of PEP. **No line above is
contingent on the sponsorship arriving** — it changes how much testing we get,
not whether the boat races.

**Note on the ESC.** The Hydra Cobra 5 HV ships with a machined water-cooled
heatsink as standard, so this is not an upgrade request — it is the same part we
already run. What we are fixing this year is the raw-water side feeding it,
which never flowed reliably.

## Schedule risk

**Both Castle parts are on back order at retail as of this week.** That is the
main schedule risk in this budget, and it is why motors and ESCs are dated
October rather than later. Ordering early, whether sponsored or purchased,
protects the winter build window. Propellers are also dated October so that prop
testing can begin as soon as the boat is back in the water.
